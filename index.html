About Us

Welcome to Chicklynne's Boneless Chicken! We are a family-owned restaurant dedicated to serving delicious boneless chicken dishes in town.
We offer fresh, crunchy, and flavorful chicken every day. We are open from 8:00AM to 8:00 PM, and close on Saturday-to-Sunday. We also sell rice bowls such as burger steak that can only be serve at our main branch Located at Sayre Highway P-2 Patag, Bagontaas, Valencia City, Bukidnon.
